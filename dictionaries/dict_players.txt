The NBA, National Basketball Association was formed in 1949, not including the history of the BAA and NBL.
At that time, there were 17 teams. The number of teams increased over time to the 30 teams we have today.

Players typically enter the NBA by showing great performances during their college basketball careers, but there are also other options,
such as professional international leagues or the G-League. Scouts are sent out to watch players perform at games and assess their
performances, inviting them for private workouts if they have potential. There is also the NBA Draft Combine, which began in 1987, 
which helped to standardize testing players' skills and athletic capabilities. 

The dataset includes 7 explanatory variables.

Unit of observation/analysis: A single player in the NBA.

Players.csv
- 'Player': Player Name (First and Last Name)

- 'Height': Player Height (cm)

- 'Weight': Player Weight (kg)

- 'College': Player's Attended College

- 'Born': Player Birth Year

- 'Birth_city': Player Birth City

- 'Birth_state': Player Birth State
